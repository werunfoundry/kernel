# Why this segment, in prose

One claim per file, and this file holds one.

Companies in this band have outgrown the spreadsheet and have not yet bought
the enterprise suite. They feel the problem weekly, they have nobody whose job
it is to fix it, and the person who notices is the one who signs.

That is the whole claim. It is here rather than in `spec` because no schema
field holds "the person who notices is the one who signs", and because the
sentence is what a generated message is allowed to cite. A claim that cannot
be cited is a claim nobody can check.

## What would falsify this

Deals in this band routinely needing a second approver. If that starts
happening, the claim is wrong and the date on the truth is a lie.
